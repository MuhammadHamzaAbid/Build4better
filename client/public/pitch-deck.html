01. The Problem

The Silent Digital Crisis of Pakistani Elders

Digital inclusion without elder-centric design has become a recipe for fear and financial fraud.

40M+
Excluded Citizens

Pakistani adults aged 50+ fear using Easypaisa, JazzCash, or bank apps due to terrifying English jargon (OTP, CVV, IBAN).

Rs. 8B+
Lost to Scams Yearly

Fake bank manager calls, lottery prizes, and urgent account blockage threats prey almost exclusively on senior citizens.

87%
Family Friction

Adult children lose patience teaching repetitive tasks. Parents feel humiliated, helpless, and afraid to try.

Talking Point: "The problem isn't that our parents cannot learn; the problem is that apps are designed for 22-year-olds in English, with zero safe practice areas."
02. The Solution

A Safe, Zero-Blame Digital Flight Simulator

We teach them how to do it themselves—never doing it for them, never shaming mistakes.

1. Interactive Hotspot Sandbox

Parents practice real tasks (e.g. Electricity Bill Payment) on authentic screens. Every tap area is calibrated with 3-tier gentle hints.

Zero-Blame Guarantee: Wrong taps trigger warm amber encouragement: "Koi baat nahi, yeh sab se hota hai!"

2. Step-by-Step AI Coach ("Madadgar AI")

Contextual AI tutor at every step that answers elder questions in natural spoken Urdu:

  • "Agar main ghalat dabba doon to paise katengay?"
  • "Reference number kaghazi bill par kahan likha hota hai?"
  • Instant answers + authentic audio playback via dual-engine TTS.
Talking Point: "Pilots practice in flight simulators before flying a plane. Our parents deserve a simulator before handling their life savings."
03. Innovation & Defense

Scam Dojo & Emergency "Mujhe Shak Hai!" Radar

Active fraud inoculation combined with a live panic button during incoming scam calls.

🥋 Scam Dojo (Training Ground)

Interactive scenarios modeled on real Pakistani fraud:

  • Benazir Income Support (BISP) lottery lures
  • Fake State Bank / Army officer calls demanding OTP
  • Interactive red-flag phrase highlighters that train visual defense

🚨 "Mujhe Shak Hai!" (Live SOS Radar)

When an elder receives a suspicious call right now in real life:

  • 3-Tap Triage: Detects OTP demands, blockage threats, or 03xx mobile numbers posing as banks.
  • Loud Voice Siren: "Foran call kaat dein! Yeh 100% fraud hai. Bank kabhi OTP nahi maangta!"
  • 1-Tap WhatsApp Alert: Immediately notifies their children with the caller info.
Talking Point: "Most apps detect scams after money is stolen. Our SOS Radar catches the scammer while the call is still ringing."
